The OpenIT wordmark.

One mark, two voices. Open in Inter Black for the engineering side — confident, precise, technical. IT in Fraunces italic for the human side — editorial, warm, considered. They sit on a shared baseline with a 2-pixel breath between them, and the colour shift from ink to terracotta carries all the personality this wordmark needs.

01Anatomy

Inter 800 Fraunces italic 500 ink → terracotta gap: 2 px @ 22 px OpenIT
Open
Inter · weight 800 · upright · tracking -0.02em · colour ink hsl(30 14% 10%)
IT
Fraunces · weight 500 · italic · tracking -0.03em · colour terracotta hsl(14 62% 50%)
Gap
2 px at the canonical 22 px size — scales proportionally with the wordmark. Baselines match exactly; no optical offset.
Sizing
Both halves share the same font-size. Fraunces optically reads ~5% smaller than Inter at the same size, which is intentional — it gives IT the editorial whisper we want.
Format
Live text only. Do not convert to outlines, do not letter-space manually, do not bake into a raster file for UI use. Ship the two webfonts.

04Clear space

OpenIT ↕ 1× ↕ 1× ↔ 1× ↔ 1×

Reserve clear space equal to one "O" cap-height on every side of the lockup. Nothing — type, icons, edges, photos, gradients — enters this zone.

05Don't

don't OpenIT

Don't set the wordmark in all caps. The Open/IT contrast comes from the case and font shift, not from shouting.

don't OpenIT

Don't italicise the whole lockup. Only the "IT" half is italic — that's the entire concept.

don't OpenIT

Don't set "IT" upright in Inter to match "Open". The Fraunces italic is what makes the wordmark.

don't OpenIT

Don't add a wide gap between "Open" and "IT". The two halves are one word, breathing across a 2-pixel seam.

don't OpenIT

Don't recolour "IT". Terracotta is the only accent — never blue, green, neon, or system-default link.

don't OpenIT

Don't weaken the weight. "Open" is always Inter 800. Anything lighter loses the engineering voice.
